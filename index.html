Customer Support Sample 04

💛 Handling a Complaint

Calm and respectful response to a concern.

Sample reply:
I'm sorry to hear that your experience hasn't met expectations. I understand how frustrating this can be. Please send your order number and a brief description of the issue so I can review it and help identify the best next step. Thank you for giving us the chance to assist.

Good practice: acknowledge the concern, avoid making promises before checking the facts, and follow the store's policy.

↩️ Return or Exchange Request

Polite response that follows store policy.

Sample reply:

Good practice: check the policy before confirming eligibility or promising a refund.
